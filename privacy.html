Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Pause is built to know as little about you as possible. This page explains what the app and this website collect, and why.

The apps you pick

Pause uses Apple's Screen Time system to lock apps. When you pick apps, Apple gives Pause sealed tokens. Pause can use a token to lock an app, but it can't see the app's name or what you do in it. Your list never leaves your phone.

No camera, no microphone

Pause doesn't use your camera or microphone. Breaks run on the honor system.

What stays on your phone

  • Your time limit and unlock task
  • How many breaks you've done
  • Your quiz answers from setup

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Pause never sees your card details.

This website

If you join the waitlist, we store your email and which phone you use. We use it only to tell you about Pause. We may use the Meta Pixel to measure our ads. You can ask us to delete your email at any time.

Children

Pause is not meant for children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: email us.
