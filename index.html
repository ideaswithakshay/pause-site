Questions

Good questions.

Can Pause see which apps I use?

No. Apple's Screen Time system handles that. Pause gets a sealed token for each app you pick. It can lock that app, but it can't read the app's name or what you did in it.

Does the camera record me?

No. Your phone checks your push-ups or stillness on the spot. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

Can I turn it off?

Yes, anytime. You can remove an app from the list or switch Pause off in the app. Pause is a speed bump, not a jail.

Is it on Android?

iPhone first. If you're on Android, join the waitlist anyway and pick Android. That tells us how many people want it.

How much will it cost?

The first 3 days are free. After that it's a yearly plan. Waitlist members hear about the launch price first.
