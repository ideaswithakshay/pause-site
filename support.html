My apps didn't lock

Open Pause and check that you picked at least one app. Apple's usage timer can run a few minutes late. That comes from Apple, not Pause.

The push-up counter missed reps

Put your phone flat on the floor, screen up, right under your face. Use a bright room. Go all the way down so your face gets close to the phone.

How do I turn Pause off?

Remove your apps from the list in Pause. You can also go to Settings → Screen Time and turn off Pause's access.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pause → Cancel. Apple handles all billing.
